Components

The five components that live inside an app sheet: button, panel, badge, field, list. Rendered from the real stylesheets with the markup to copy. This page loads no shell, which is the point: the components work on an ordinary page, and the launcher in the demo works around them.

Button

Four intents, one size modifier, and a block modifier that is the normal shape in a 390px column. The press is one pixel of travel and the shadow going flat.

Panel

A surface inside a sheet. The muted variant recedes; use it for the thing the reader compares against, not the thing you want chosen.

The month line

On the first of the month the last month collapses into a single line of numbers, which is the only history the app keeps.

Quiet reminders

One notification a day at a time you pick, or none at all. There is no second reminder that asks whether you are sure.

Badge

Ink text on a pale fill, always. Meaning lives in the hue and legibility never depends on it. The same four fills tint the app plates on the home screen, under the same rule.

Field

Label, input, hint, error. The input is 16px and 48px tall, which is what stops iOS Safari zooming on focus and what a thumb needs.
