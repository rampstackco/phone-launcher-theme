About

Two people and a wheel.

We built this for ourselves in 2024 and put it on the store when a third person asked for a copy.

Most habit apps grow into life-management platforms with dashboards, streak insurance and a paid tier for graphs. We went the other way. Pawl holds a small number of things and counts them, and every feature request that would add a second screen has been turned down so far.

The name is the part that stops a ratchet slipping. It is a small piece of metal that does one job, which is what we are aiming at.

How it works

Three parts. That is the product.

Each one does its job and stops. Nothing here has a roadmap to become a platform.

The wheel

Up to five things you are keeping up. Five is the cap and it is not configurable, because the sixth thing is how people stop using trackers.

The tooth

One tap marks today. Miss a day and the count pauses where it is; it does not reset, and nothing sends you a guilt notification about it.

The rule

On the first of the month the last month collapses into a single line of numbers. That line is the only history the app keeps, and it fits on one screen.

Streaks

The count, and what it refuses to do.

A streak is a number of days. It is not a score, a level, or a thing you can buy back.

  • Pauses on a missed day The number holds instead of dropping to zero yes
  • Freeze tokens to buy The mechanic that turns a habit into a currency no
  • Leaderboards Your flossing is nobody's competition no
  • Backdating One day back, once, for the day you forgot the phone 1 day

The one-day allowance is the most argued-about line in the app. It stays because forgetting to record something is not the same as not doing it.

Privacy

It stays on the phone.

There is no account, so there is no server holding a list of what you are trying to do about your health.

  • Account required no
  • Analytics SDK none
  • Sync your iCloud
  • Export CSV

Sync runs through your own iCloud or Google Drive folder, so the file is yours and we never see it. Deleting the app deletes the data, which is worth knowing before you delete the app.
